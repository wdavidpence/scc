**SC1 original intro:** salvagers (Jem, Joeyray) picking through wrecks near Chau Sara → conversation cut off by a cloaked Protoss ship incinerating them → hard cut to the game's title card. Cold open: cosmic horror from the victim's POV, zero exposition, ~60s.
**Wasteland Patrol micro-cinematic:** two Confederates in a jeep run over a zergling; the sergeant starts "zerglings are never alone unless…" — interrupted by zerglinging up all around them.-setup/payoff in 15 seconds.
