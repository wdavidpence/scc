# PILLARS

## P1 — Immediate Command Clarity
Every player action produces visible and audible confirmation within 100 ms; selection, intent, affordability, danger, and outcomes remain understandable without prior genre knowledge.

## P2 — Terrain Is Strategy
Rendered geography and simulation topology are identical: mountains are impassable, ramps are explicit, valleys route armies, high ground changes information and combat, and every legal destination has a readable path.

## P3 — Deterministic Depth at Scale
A fixed-step, data-driven simulation supports reproducible replays, lockstep multiplayer, headless testing, and 400 active units under an 8 ms simulation budget without sacrificing tactical control.

## Signature Mechanic — Command Echo
The player records a 4–12 second sequence of spatial squad commands as a reusable intent glyph, then applies it context-relatively to another squad; execution, interruption, visibility, and counterplay are deterministic and readable.
