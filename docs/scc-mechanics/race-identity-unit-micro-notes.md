# Race Identity and Unit Micro Patterns (SCC Research Notes)

## Terran
- Distinct feel: Mechanical, versatile, defensive with strong positional play; bio/mech mix allows flexible responses to threats. [Video ref: SC2 Terran fundamentals]
- Common micro: Stim timing for bio pushes, siege tank positioning for zone control, medivac drops for harassment. [SC:BW/2 pro play]
- Engagement/disengagement: Use stim to engage aggressively then kite back with medivacs; disengage via dropship pickup or bunker retreat. [Micro tutorial videos]
- Unit control skill: Marine splitting vs splash, ghost EMP/nuke timing, raven auto-turret placement. [Pro ladder VODs]
- Repeated micro cues: Stim pack activation sound/visual, siege mode toggle animation, medivac pickup radius highlight.

## Zerg
- Distinct feel: Swarm aggression, rapid adaptation, economy through larva/inject; high mobility and numbers overwhelm. [Zerg macro-micro balance videos]
- Common micro: Drone inject cycles, overlord spread for vision, mutalisk/baneling micro for hit-and-run. [SC2 Zerg guide series]
- Engagement/disengagement: Baneling suicide engages, mutas disengage by spreading or burrowing; roach/ravager kiting. [Pro Zerg micro analysis]
- Unit control skill: Ling/bane surround execution, viper abduct timing, brood lord positioning. [Tournament VODs]
- Repeated micro cues: Larva inject animation, creep spread visual, baneling detonation radius.

## Protoss
- Distinct feel: Tech-heavy, shield/armor focus, warp-in mobility, strong early defense with cannons/zealots. [Protoss identity deep dives]
- Common micro: Stalker blink micro for kiting, zealot charge timing, high templar storm placement. [SC2 Protoss micro tutorials]
- Engagement/disengagement: Blink in/out for harass, warp prism pickup for disengage; carrier focus fire with interceptors. [Pro Protoss play]
- Unit control skill: Colossus positioning to avoid anti-air, disruptor nova timing, mothership recall. [Advanced micro VODs]
- Repeated micro cues: Shield recharge glow, blink teleport effect, psi storm visual/audio cue.

## Cross-Race Micro Patterns
- Engagement: Focus fire priority, surround execution, ability timing windows. [General RTS micro comps]
- Disengagement: Kiting, pickup/ warp, terrain abuse. [All race VOD analysis]
- Skill expression: Precise ability use under pressure, multi-unit control (split, focus, retreat). [Pro player micro breakdowns]
- Cues: Animation tells for abilities, sound design for cooldowns, visual feedback on buffs/debuffs. [Video reference set 1-10]
